// HeartWrench Level Mechanic Design Methodology - MDC 2025 //
//////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////

1. Inspiration. Start with a concept that requires the player to use at least one core ability. 
   Inspiration can come from anywhere:
   -An image
   -A phrase or lyric
   -An existing mechanic you want to tweak
   -A shower thought
   -Other mediums: a painting, a movie, a novel, a song, etc.

2. Narrative. Review the concept for narrative interest. Is the action interesting, and aligned with the tone of the world?

3. Dimensions. After step 1 and 2 you have gameplay in one dimension. Build out the gameplay dimensions of the mechanic. 
   For example, in an action platformer, a mechanic could impact ALL of the below:
   -Player movement/position
   -Combat
   -Currency
   -Local setting 
   -Overworld setting 
   A mechanic that impacts gameplay in multiple dimensions is a deeper mechanic. 2 dimensions seems comfortable for most mechanics.  

*** Iterate on Steps 4 & 5 in conjunction with Playtesting ***

4. Technical calibration. 
   Ensure that the mechanic:
   -Communicates its rules quickly and clearly
   -Behaves reliably and consistently

5. Skill calibration.
   Balance the mechanic so that:
   -A low skill player can comfortably utilize the mechanic to level completion.
   -A higher skill player can bolster knowledge and precision to gain small additional utility from mechanics.

**************************************************************    

6. Polish. Finally, design art, animation and game feel in alignment with the game's art and feel direction.
