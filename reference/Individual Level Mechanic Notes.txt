*** Level Idea ***

// Platforming Mechanics:

-Bounce Bells. Striking a bounce bell ends the player's attack state and lobs the player up in the air, even if they are out of jumps. 
 Bounce Bells may or may not remove any forward momentum from the attack.

-Track Bells. Bounce bells that follow horizontal and vertical tracks like an autoplatform.

-Track Spikes. Spike obstacles that follow horizontal and vertical tracks like an autoplatform.

// Enemies:

-Shift Blocks. Move on the X and Y axis similar to Thwomps. Crushing the player will kill them. 
 Player can touch all the sides safely and can ride the top. The eyes follow the player.

-Ground Ghouls.
