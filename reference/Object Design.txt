Start with an object/visual that you want in the game.

1. Decide what the object is.

2. Look at reference photos.

3. Before drafting, consider what temperature the object adds to the environment.

4. Before drafting, decide on the color. The color palette should adhere to the established conventions of the area and the desired temperature.

5. Before drafting, consider what shape language the object should use. Should it adhere to the shape conventions of the area or compliment them?

6. Before drafting, consider what topics or themes the object can represent in addition to being itself.

7. Draft the silhouette in one color. Review the primary silhouette and negative shapes for shape appeal.

8. Finalize the proportions.

9. Return to the established color palette and "color" + shade. 
   Find opportunities for shape appeal and adhere to your established shading conventions.
