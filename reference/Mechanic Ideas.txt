= Platforming =

-Player doesn't have a strong drift speed like in Mario, which is largely necessary
 on the GBA. Consider including level mechanics that bounce, launch, propel, or otherwise
 move the player horizontally to compensate for low inherent drift.

-Player max jump height is 4 tiles. Don't give the player jumps higher than 3 tiles. 

= Enemies/Combat =
-Basic RPS with Roll/Slash/Jump
-Build on RPS difficulty with enemy and terrain based mixups

Slash:
-Hammer attacks with lots of knockback
-Grounded hammer: 1 regular, but mighty swing. The bread and butter.
-Aerial hammer: 2 swings, first swing has low knockback and can lead 
                into the second or into a grounded hammer.
-Roll hammer: a spin along the ground, hitting on both sides of the player.
              Most useful for attacking after rolling through an enemy 
              without turning around.

-Enemies take wall splat damage?

-Enemy types?
-Enemies that are exclusively combat
-Enemies that are platforming mechanics while alive
-Enemies that create a platforming mechanic when killed
-Cinematic sequence enemies
-Bosses

= Progression =
- Each level contains a set amount of currency.
- Currency can be spent to unlock checkpoints in levels.
- Player can play faster and skip currency, but runs a higher risk of losing progress.
- Exploration (overworld and in level) pays for checkpoints via currency rewards.
- Progress is lost on death. Currency is permanent and not lost on death.
- The more currency held, the higher damage the player does?
