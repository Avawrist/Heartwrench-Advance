=== Game Mechanics ===
======================

= Platforming =

-Player max jump height is 4 tiles. Don't give the player jumps higher than 3 tiles. 
-player max jump width is 5 tiles. Don't give the player jumps wider than 4 tiles.

-Rooms in general will scroll horizontally. 
-Keep steep climbing to a minimum because of the GBA's low vertical resolution.

-Add mechanics that require the player to attack to move further vertically than their jump allows.
-Elevator platform?

== Drops & Rewards ==

-It's important to give the player short term rewards for difficult platforming, combat and exploration.
-Rewards need to feel meaningful to the player.

- Gems - 
The general currency, used to pay for checkpoints, and player palettes. Brainstorm more.

- HP - 
HP is nice in a pinch. HP should not drop from enemies or the player may opt to farm. 

- Champion Coins -
Every level will contain 3 OPTIONAL Champion Coins, each a different color and each 
"awarded" to the player for completing a special task in level.

The blue coin will be awarded for finding secret areas or solving a simple puzzle.
The red coin will be awarded for winning a difficult (optional) combat scenario.
The green coin will be awarded for overcoming a difficult platforming section.

= Enemies/Combat =
-Basic RPS with Roll/Slash/Jump
-Build on RPS difficulty with enemy and terrain based mixups

Slash:
-Hammer attacks with lots of knockback
-Grounded hammer: 1 regular, but mighty swing. The bread and butter.
-Aerial hammer: 2 swings, first swing has low knockback and can lead 
                into the second or into a grounded hammer.
-Roll hammer: a spin along the ground, hitting on both sides of the player.
              Most useful for attacking after rolling through an enemy 
              without turning around.

-Enemies take wall splat damage

-Enemy types:
-Enemies that are exclusively combat
-Enemies that are platforming mechanics while alive
-Enemies that create a platforming mechanic when killed
-Cinematic sequence enemies
-Bosses

= Progression =
- Each level contains a set amount of currency.
- Currency can be spent to unlock checkpoints in levels.
- Player can play faster and skip currency, but runs a higher risk of losing progress.
- Exploration (overworld and in level) pays for checkpoints via currency rewards.
- Progress is lost on death. Currency is permanent and not lost on death.
- The more currency held, the higher damage the player does?
