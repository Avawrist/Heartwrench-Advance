// 3rd Gen Console Era (NES/Famicom, Sega Master System, Atari 7800) //
///////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////

// Consumables (Temporary):
STA - Extra lives
STA - Hit Points * 
STA - Damage Points
STA - Mana Points
STA - Ammo
STA - Movement & Weapon Abilities

// Ability Upgrades + Equipment (Permanent):
LTP - Hit Points, Damage Points, Mana Points, Ammo, Movement & Weapon Abilities

// Other:
LTP - Key Items / Quest Items
LTP - Keys
LTP - Experience Points
LTP - Collectables
LTP/STA - Currency - stored and redeemable for: consumables, 
						ability upgrades/equipment, 
						information,
						& visual change.

/////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////
// 4th Gen Console Era (SNES/Super Famicom, Sega Genesis, SNK Neo Geo) //
/////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////

LTP/STA - Crafting Resources - Resources that can be combined to receive consumables, 
                               ability upgrades/equipment, & other.

			       Resources may be currency, consumables, equipment, or key items.

                               A + A -> B 
			       B > A + A

			       A + B -> C
			       C > A + B
