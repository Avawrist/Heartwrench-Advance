** Minish Cap Spritework Notes **

The Workflow:
1. Start by finding the biggest shapes
2. Using these big shapes, build a silhouette with shape appeal
3. Now begin to describe the forms using primarily round subshapes
4. Small details can be added in now, taking care not to overcrowd the sprite.
5. For rounder forms, a soft edge can be added to the outline.
   Outlines are lighter near the top of the sprite and darker near the base,
   indicating a top light.
6. A full black outline can be used for areas that require higher contrast to stand out.

Essential Tips:
* Work off of a public color palette until you are comfortable picking colors
* The Minish Cap art is all in top-down perspective. 
* Do not try to describe each unique surface normal with a different color.
  Changes in surface can be implied, and contained within a single color. 
* Avoid blocky forms with sharp angles, these are harder to describe at a low
  resolution. Sharp angles usually require an explicit color change to describe,
  and can crowd a small sprite with too many noisy patches of color.
